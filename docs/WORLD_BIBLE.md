# Cardbound — Original IP World Bible

Status: **Pre-production canon v0.1**

This document is the creative source of truth for converting the prototype into an original commercial game. Names marked **working** are deliberately not treated as final brand/trademark decisions until clearance is completed.

## 1. Core fantasy

Reality is held together by **Bindings**: mystical patterns that define creatures, tools, places, resources, techniques, and powers. Rare people called **Binders** can capture those patterns as physical cards and manifest what they have bound.

A card is therefore not merely a collectible. It is permission, knowledge, and power made tangible.

- Bind a creature → you can track and challenge it.
- Bind a resource → you can recognize and harvest it.
- Bind equipment → you can manifest and equip it.
- Bind a facility → you can operate that facility during an activity.
- Bind a higher power → you can form an allegiance and earn its favor.
- Foil cards represent **Perfect Bindings**, exceptionally stable versions of a pattern.
- Shredding breaks unwanted Bindings into **Essence Fragments**, which can be recombined into targeted new Bindings.

This fiction directly explains the game's collection, unlock, duplicate, crafting, equipment, activity, foil, and allegiance mechanics.

## 2. Player identity

The player is a newly awakened **Binder** building a personal **Codex**. Progress is driven by discovery rather than a fixed hero storyline. The player's collection is effectively their character sheet.

Primary player verbs:

**Discover → Bind → Equip → Venture → Master → Refine → Collect**

## 3. Setting

### The world

Working term: **The Shattered Reach**.

The Reach is a broad fantasy world fractured long ago by an event called **the Sundering**, when the underlying Bindings of reality became visible and unstable. Civilization survives around stable regions while wild territories contain creatures, resources, ruins, seas, and anomalies whose patterns can be captured.

The setting should support many future biomes without requiring a single linear continent:

- green frontier / old forests
- mountains and deep mines
- coastlines and open sea
- desert and volcanic wastes
- marsh and corrupted wilderness
- frozen regions
- ancient ruins
- astral / endgame regions

Final world and region names will be selected only after name clearance.

## 4. Tone and visual identity

**Tone:** adventurous, mysterious, collectible, slightly weathered fantasy — not grimdark and not cartoon parody.

**Visual pillars:**

1. Illustrated collectible cards are the visual centerpiece.
2. UI resembles a Binder's field codex, vault, maps, engraved metal, parchment, and magical sigils.
3. Equipment is readable at mobile size with strong silhouettes.
4. Monsters should feel like members of coherent ecological families rather than isolated random designs.
5. Higher rarity means increased craftsmanship, magical complexity, framing, animation, and VFX — not simply a different border color.
6. Perfect/Foil Bindings use animated etched light, refractive glyphs, or moving pattern-lines rather than copying physical TCG foil treatments.

## 5. Combat disciplines

Keep three broad combat disciplines because they are intuitive genre concepts:

- **Melee** — close-range weapons, armor, blocking, heavy impact.
- **Ranged** — bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, precision, mobility.
- **Magic** — focuses, staves, grimoires, elemental/arcane techniques.

Future combat differentiation should use weaknesses, resistances, weapon traits, equipment synergies, and boss mechanics rather than three cosmetic copies of the same progression.

## 6. Professions

Prototype skills become original professions while retaining familiar fantasy activities where appropriate.

- **Woodcraft** — trees, rare timber, groves, lumber facilities.
- **Mining** — ore, stone, crystal, deep deposits.
- **Fishing** — freshwater, coastal, deep-water catches.
- **Sailing** — ports, charting, salvage, trawling, naval encounters, trials.
- **Huntsmanship** — specialized contracts against increasingly dangerous creature families; replaces the prototype's Slayer terminology and progression identity.

Potential future professions: Smithing, Artifice, Cooking, Alchemy, Foraging, Runecraft-like original magic crafting, Exploration.

## 7. Equipment progression

Do not perform one-for-one RuneScape renames. Build equipment around original material cultures and combat identities.

Working material ladder:

1. **Iron** — common practical gear.
2. **Steel** — refined martial gear.
3. **Gloamsteel** — dark alloy responsive to Bindings.
4. **Wyrmforged** — high-tier material produced using draconic/monstrous essence.
5. **Starforged** — rare celestial material.
6. **Relic** — named ancient equipment with unique traits.
7. **Mythic** — exceptional endgame artifacts, not a generic metal tier.

These names are working terminology and require clearance before branding/publication.

Equipment slots remain genre-standard where useful: head, neck, body, legs, hands, feet, cape/back, weapon, off-hand, ammunition/focus, ring, and profession tool.

## 8. Creature design

Creatures are organized into families so card collection and Huntsmanship can build recognizable sets.

Initial family concepts:

- **Briarborn** — plant/forest creatures.
- **Fangkin** — predatory beasts and pack hunters.
- **Hollowed** — undead or Binding-damaged beings.
- **Emberclad** — volcanic creatures.
- **Deepkin** — cave and subterranean creatures.
- **Tideborn** — coastal/ocean creatures.
- **Aetherials** — magical anomalies and late-game entities.
- **Colossi** — giant/boss-class creatures.

Bosses should have original silhouettes, names, mechanics, habitats, lore, and reward identities rather than corresponding one-to-one with prototype bosses.

## 9. Higher powers / allegiance

Working collective term: **The Ascendants**.

Ascendants are powerful entities whose Bindings are so vast that obtaining their cards permits a Binder to enter an allegiance rather than manifesting the entity directly.

Design target: 8 original Ascendants, each supporting a gameplay identity.

Provisional domains:

- Forge / battle / resilience
- Wilds / growth / harvesting
- Tides / travel / Sailing
- Veil / death / Huntsmanship
- Stars / Magic / discovery
- Hunt / Ranged / precision
- Fortune / packs / fragments
- Oaths / defense / mastery

Rules retained from the prototype:

- A normal Ascendant card unlocks favor progression.
- Maximum two active allegiances.
- Swapping a normal allegiance resets that Ascendant's favor.
- A Perfect/Foil Ascendant Binding preserves favor when swapped out.
- Favor activities require active player interaction and cannot be idled.

Exact names, symbols, lore, and benefits will be designed as an original pantheon after clearance research.

## 10. Packs and rarity

Working rarity ladder:

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- **Ascendant** — special rarity reserved for higher-power cards and similarly exceptional content.

Pack tiers should have original names and packaging rather than metal tiers inherited from the prototype. Pack identity should communicate progression and expected rarity without mimicking another game's item ladder.

Potential structure:

- Wayfarer's Cache
- Adventurer's Cache
- Binder's Reliquary
- Grand Reliquary
- Mythic Reliquary

Working names only.

## 11. Duplicate economy

Prototype mechanics become part of the Binding fiction:

- Duplicate normal cards → **Essence Fragments** by rarity.
- Duplicate Perfect/Foil cards → **Prismatic Essence**.
- Enough fragments can be recombined into a chosen category within the same rarity, subject to balancing.
- Target crafting should reduce bad-luck frustration without replacing pack discovery.

## 12. Bank / collection terminology

Working terminology:

- **Vault** — owned equipment and usable cards; replaces RuneScape-specific bank presentation.
- **Codex** — complete collection encyclopedia.
- **Loadouts** — saved equipment configurations.
- **Bindery** — fragment recombination/crafting interface.
- **Venture** — activity selection umbrella.

Names remain subject to UX testing and clearance.

## 13. Endgame structure

Raids become original **Expeditions**: multi-encounter activities that require the player to have discovered/bound the key encounter cards before entry.

Challenge-gauntlet content becomes **Trials**. Each Trial has an original creature roster and final encounter rather than reproducing another game's encounter sequence.

Design principles:

- collection requirements should create goals, not arbitrary walls;
- encounter cards should teach players what they will face;
- bosses should reward build planning and combat-discipline choices;
- endgame should create uses for broad collections rather than only one best loadout.

## 14. Commercial design principles

The original-IP version is designed to support commercial release without depending on third-party game IP.

Preferred monetization direction:

- free download;
- full core progression available through play;
- optional one-time Supporter upgrade;
- cosmetic card backs, Vault themes, profile customization, and visual effects;
- optional rewarded ads if they improve rather than interrupt play;
- avoid mandatory interstitial ads;
- avoid selling direct combat power;
- avoid real-money randomized packs during initial launch.

## 15. IP separation rules

Production content must not contain third-party game-specific:

- names or trademarks;
- characters, deities, monsters, bosses, locations, factions, quests, or lore;
- item/equipment names that are distinctive to another game;
- sprites, icons, screenshots, textures, sounds, music, models, or extracted assets;
- copied encounter compositions;
- copied UI layouts distinctive enough to identify the source game;
- external image URLs serving third-party game assets.

Generic fantasy concepts can be used, but their expression should be Cardbound's own.

## 16. Naming policy

**Cardbound itself remains a working title until trademark/name clearance is completed.** Do not commission expensive logo/marketing work around any final title before clearance.

For every major coined name:

1. search general web results;
2. search major app/game storefronts;
3. search relevant trademark databases in intended launch territories;
4. search domains/social handles;
5. record the result in the IP conversion ledger;
6. obtain professional trademark advice before a commercial launch if the brand is being relied upon.

## 17. Canon rule

If game code, marketing copy, art prompts, or design notes conflict with this document after the original-IP conversion begins, this World Bible wins unless it is intentionally revised first.
